Small objects in unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) images provide limited visual evidence and occur amid clutter and scale variation. We present MPSMA-DEIM, an adaptation of DEIM-D-FINE that combines a Multi-branch Progressive Stem (MPS) with Multi-scale Context-aware Attention (MSCA). The stem merges detail, downsampling and high-frequency branches into stride-four features. The attention design uses pooled context at an intermediate stage and dilated context at the deepest stage, with SimAM-derived energy gates. We compare the complete n configuration with its original baseline over 320 retained training rounds on 6,471 images using seed 0, FP32 and batch size 4. Evaluation uses all 548 VisDrone2019 validation images under frozen local COCO-style settings. The final-round exponential moving average (EMA) achieves average precision (AP) of 0.2255 and small-object AP (APs) of 0.1539, compared with baseline values of 0.1902 and 0.1215. The absolute differences are 3.53 and 3.23 percentage points. The complete model has 7.54 million trainable parameters, compared with 3.74 million for the baseline. The comparison includes documented checkpoint continuations. Component-specific effects are being evaluated separately.
