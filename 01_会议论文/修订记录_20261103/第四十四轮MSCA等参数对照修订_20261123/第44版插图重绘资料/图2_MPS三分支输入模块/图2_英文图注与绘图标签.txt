图 2　MPS 三分支输入模块

这幅图要表达什么
说明同一张图像的主路、细节路和高频路怎样对齐、拼接、加权，再输出步幅为 4 的特征。

可复制的英文绘图标签
• Input X · 3 × 640 × 640
• Main branch · 3 × 3 conv (stride 2) → 3 × 3 conv (stride 1) · 16 channels
• Detail branch · two 3 × 3 convs (stride 1) → 2 × 2 average pooling (stride 2) · 8 channels
• High-pass branch · X − AvgPool3×3(X) → 1 × 1 conv → 3 × 3 depthwise conv → bilinear resize · 4 channels
• Concatenate [main, detail, high-pass] → Z · 28 × 320 × 320
• Spatial gate A(Z) · 1 × 1 conv (28 → 2) → BN → SiLU → 7 × 7 conv (2 → 1) → sigmoid
• Residual weighting · Z ⊙ (1 + A(Z))
• Fusion · 1 × 1 conv (28 → 32) → 3 × 3 conv (32 → 16)
• Final 3 × 3 conv (stride 2) → 16 × 160 × 160

重绘时核对
从 Z 分一路去求 A，另一路把 Z 接到加权节点。A 的空间尺寸是 320 × 320，单通道权重沿 28 个通道广播。

第 44 版英文原图注
Fig. 2. MPS for HGNetv2-B0 with H = W = 640 for the reported inputs. The three branches are concatenated, followed by residual weighting, fusion convolutions and a final stride-two convolution. BN denotes batch normalization.
