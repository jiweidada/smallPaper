Minimum Delay Object Detection From Video (2019)
https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.11092
https://donglao.github.io/files/Lao_Minimum_Delay_Object_Detection_From_Video_ICCV_2019_paper.pdf
online first-detection delay under accuracy/false-alarm constraints; accumulates single-frame detector evidence across time using quickest detection. Direct overlap with generic unconfirmed-evidence accumulation.
First-acquisition delay plus temporal accumulation alone is not a new contribution.
核实范围：primary abstract and author PDF search text including Algorithm2

A Delay Metric for Video Object Detection: What Average Precision Fails to Tell (2019)
https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.06368
https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ICCV_2019/papers/Mao_A_Delay_Metric_for_Video_Object_Detection_What_Average_Precision_ICCV_2019_paper.pdf
AD measures detection delay and considers false-alarm constraints; AP alone misses temporal behavior.
Reporting time to first detection or saying AP misses delay is already established.
核实范围：primary abstract and CVF fulltext extraction

Seq-NMS for Video Object Detection (2016)
https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.08465

nearby high-scoring detections boost weak detections through sequence-level postprocessing.
Temporal score propagation is an existing baseline, not a proposed innovation.
核实范围：primary abstract

https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S003132032600004X
