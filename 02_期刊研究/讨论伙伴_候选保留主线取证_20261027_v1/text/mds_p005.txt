V . EXPERIMENTS
A. EXPERIMENT SETTINGS AND IMPLEMENTATION
DETAILS
Building upon prior work, we evaluate our method on the
COCO 2017 dataset [11] using standard AP metrics: mAP,
AP50, AP 75, AP S, AP M , and AP L. All experiments were
conducted using an ImageNet [36]-pretrained ResNet-50
backbone [37] with a batch size of 16 on 8 NVIDIA RTX
4090 GPUs. We use the AdamW optimizer [38] with a
learning rate of2×10 −4 and weight decay of1×10 −4.
