Query Routing.Table 2 presents the average number of queries passing through
the layers. Additionally, Fig. 4 shows the query distributions within the proposed
query routing module. For EEQRF and EEQRCA, skewed Gaussian distributions
are observed: EEQRF tends to route all update queries through the entire layer,
whereas EEQRCA tends to bypass the layer for queries not requiring updates. In
contrast, EEQRSA follows a more normal distribution, highlighting the impor-
tance of information sharing through the SA layer. As indicated in Table 3, this
results in a quicker model despite the higher MACs.
