3 False Negatives and Impact of Object Characteristics
Detectors may incur a false negative by assigning a low conﬁdence to an object or by
missing it completely. Intuitively, an object may be difﬁcult to detect due to occlusion,
truncation, small size, or unusual viewpoint. In this section, we measure the sensitivity
of detectors to these characteristics and others and also try to answer why so many
objects (typically about 40%) are not detected with even very low conﬁdence.
