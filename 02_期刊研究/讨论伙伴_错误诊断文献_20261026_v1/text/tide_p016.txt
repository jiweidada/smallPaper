∆APa,b =APa,b−AP ∆AP a +∆APb =APa +APb− 2AP (8)
Then we rearrange the terms for the left equation to get it in terms of AP:
AP =APa,b−∆APa,b (9)
Then, substitute 1 AP into the right equation in Eq. 8 to get

We can then group APa−AP and−(APa,b−APb) and substitute them with the
