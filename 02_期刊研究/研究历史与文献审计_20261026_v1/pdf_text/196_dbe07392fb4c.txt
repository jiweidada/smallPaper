Highlights
Accurate Shift Invariant Convolutional Neural Networks Using Gaussian-
Hermite Moments
Jaspreet Singh, Petra Bosilj, Grzegorz Cielniak
•Gaussian-Hermite Sampling is introduced to achieve accurate shift in-
variance in CNNs.
•It provides guarantied invariance at each convolutional layer without
extra training.
•Acts as a built-in low-pass filter, reducing aliasing and noise sensitivity.
•Achieves 100% consistency for shifts on CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and
MNIST-rot datasets.
