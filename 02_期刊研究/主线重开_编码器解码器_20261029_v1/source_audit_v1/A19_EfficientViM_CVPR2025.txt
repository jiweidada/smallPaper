                             Abstract

For the deployment of neural networks in resource-
constrained environments, prior works have built
lightweight architectures with convolution and attention
for capturing local and global dependencies, respectively.
Recently, the state space model (SSM) has emerged as an
effective operation for global interaction with its favorable
linear computational cost in the number of tokens. To
harness the efficacy of SSM, we introduce Efficient Vision
Mamba (EfficientViM), a novel architecture built on
hidden state mixer-based state space duality (HSM-SSD)
