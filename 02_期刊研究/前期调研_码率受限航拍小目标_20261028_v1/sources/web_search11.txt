Chengjie Dai received his bachelor degree of Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from University of Science and Technology Beijing, P. R. China, in 2020. Currently, he is pursuing the doctor's degree at the School of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Zhejiang University. His research interests include computer vision, image compression and super resolution.

Tiantian Song is currently an undergraduate student in Mathematics and Statistics within the Department of Mathematics at the University of Manchester. Her interests lie in the interface between applied mathematics, statistics and data analytics. She is particularly interested in implementing algorithms in image processing and analysis.

Qiang Chen graduated from the East China Normal University in 2012, majored in the Digital Image Processing and Face Recognition, and received a master's degree in Systems Analysis and Integration. Currently, he is a senior cloud architect at Shanghai FinShine Technology Co., Ltd., and his current research interests mainly include research on cloud native technologies, such as K8S, edge computing, and hyper-convergence infrastructure. He has worked for companies such as Intel, IBM, Lenovo, and iQiYi etc.

HanShen Gong graduated from Shanghai University in 2007 with a bachelor degree in Electronic Communication Engineering. Currently, he is the director of the Cloud and Security Division at Shanghai FinShine Technology Co., Ltd., where he is responsible for the development of trustable cloud platforms and artificial intelligence training platforms. His research interests mainly involve large-scale heterogeneous data parallel processing systems, and he has obtained multiple research achievements in this field.

Bowei Yang received the B.S. and Ph.D. degrees in computer science from Zhejiang University, China, in 2006 and 2011, respectively. In 2016, he started the AI assisted wireless communications researches in The University of Sheffield, U.K., and was promoted to the Associate Professor at the School of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Zhejiang University, China. His research interests include mobile edge computing, reinforcement learning, and learned image compression.

Guanghua Song received the B.S. degree in Cmputer Science from Nanjing University of Science and Technology, China, in 1989, and the M.S. and Ph. D. degrees in Computer Science from Zhejiang University, China, in 1992 and 2003, respectively. He is currently a full Professor with School of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Zhejiang University, China.
